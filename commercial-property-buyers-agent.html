Where the opportunity sits

See what the property could become. Understand what it takes to get there.

Under-market rent, vacancy or dated presentation can create an opportunity when the purchase price, tenant demand, improvement costs and execution plan support it.

Rent below market

Passing rent sits under what comparable leases support.

  • Market rent evidence for the precinct
  • Review mechanisms and lease terms

Lease expiry or vacancy

A short lease or vacant space that narrows the buyer pool.

  • Tenant demand for the space
  • Leasing costs, incentives and downtime

Dated presentation

A well-located building that no longer presents to its position.

  • Scope and cost of the works
  • The rent the works can support

Before buying, assess what can change, what it will cost, how long it may take and whether the purchase remains workable if the improvement is delayed.

Not every value-add opportunity stacks up, and not every investor needs one.

Acquisition and ownership

Buy with a plan for ownership.

The purchase decision and the ownership strategy belong together. Assess what the asset delivers today, what could improve and the work required to get there.

  1. 01 · SELECT

    Find the right fit

    Establish the acquisition brief and assess suitable opportunities against your objectives, budget and ownership requirements.

  2. 02 · PLAN

    Test the opportunity

    Assess income quality, lease events and practical improvements. For value-add opportunities, consider costs, timing, tenant demand and cash flow before relying on the upside.

  3. 03 · ACQUIRE

    Negotiate with evidence

    Use market evidence and a clear commercial position to negotiate price and terms, with due diligence coordinated alongside your solicitor and relevant consultants.

  4. 04 · EXECUTE

    Carry the plan forward

    Separately appointed asset management is available throughout Queensland to oversee the agreed ownership strategy and coordinate value-add initiatives. Property management and leasing are available in Brisbane.

Ongoing services are not included in the acquisition fee and are scoped and agreed separately. Specialist legal, building, valuation and finance advice comes from the relevant advisers. No purchase savings, yields or returns are promised.

Common questions

Questions buyers ask

How are fees agreed?

Nick will explain the proposed scope, engagement fee and acquisition fee before you appoint Represent Commercial. Your written appointment will set out when fees are earned and payable.

I have already found a property. Can you still help?

Yes. Nick can assess the property, coordinate specialist due diligence and negotiate price and terms on your behalf. It helps to make contact before you commit to a price or sign a contract.

Do I need a value-add strategy?

No. Many investors buy for stable income. The initial session considers whether a value-add approach suits your objectives, capital and capacity for leasing or works.

Who oversees the works and leasing after settlement?

Asset management is available throughout Queensland to oversee the ownership strategy and coordinate agreed value-add initiatives. For Brisbane assets, property management and leasing can also be separately agreed. Each service is scoped and agreed separately.

Do you work across Queensland?

Yes. Represent Commercial acts for investors buying industrial, retail, office and mixed-use property across Queensland.

Who carries out specialist due diligence?

Your legal, valuation, building, finance and tax advisers carry out their own specialist work. Nick coordinates with them where needed and brings their findings into the assessment and negotiation.
