How this is calculated

Review basis

A fixed review applies the percentage you enter. A CPI review applies your assumed inflation rate. A greater-of review applies whichever of the two is higher, which is the figure used for every review in the model — the clause is not assessed separately at each review against a varying inflation series.

Timing and frequency

Year one is the rent as entered. A review is applied on each anniversary at the frequency you select, so a rent reviewed every three years holds for three years and then steps. Rates are entered per annum and compounded across the review interval, which is how a multi-year fixed review is ordinarily expressed.

Total rent over the term

The sum of each year’s rent across the modelled term, undiscounted. It is a cash total, not a present value.

Effective growth

The compound annual rate that takes the commencing rent to the final-year rent over the term.

What is excluded

Outgoings, GST, incentives, rent-free periods, market reviews, ratchet provisions, vacancy and make-good are not modelled. A market review can move rent in either direction and cannot be projected from a rate assumption. Where a lease contains a market review, treat these figures as the fixed and indexed path only.

A review clause is negotiable.

The basis, the frequency and the ratchet are all decided before the lease is signed, and they compound for the whole term. Represent negotiates review structures on behalf of owners and tenants with the market evidence to support the position.
