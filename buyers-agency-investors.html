Value-add detail

Testing a value-add opportunity

Not every acquisition needs a value-add strategy. Where one suits you and the property, it needs more than a higher rent assumption. Before buying, test what can change, what it will cost, how long it will take and whether the purchase still makes sense if the improvement is delayed.

  1. 01

    Income and lease quality

    Test passing rent against market evidence, lease terms, incentives, vacancy and recoverable outgoings. Model the cost and timing of capturing rental reversion.

  2. 02

    Practical improvements

    Assess presentation, access, layout and capital works against tenant demand. Allow for approvals, consultant advice, works costs and disruption.

  3. 03

    Cash flow before uplift

    Model acquisition costs, holding costs, leasing costs and staged works alongside income. Test the downside before relying on a future value increase.

  4. 04

    Actions, budget and sequence

    Define the actions, budget and sequence before buying, including leasing, rental reviews and property improvements.

Common questions

Buying commercial property as an investor

How do I know if I am paying too much?

By testing the asking price against comparable sale evidence, the passing rent against market rent, and the outgoings recovery against what the lease actually permits. A price can look acceptable on yield and still be too high if the rent sits above market or the recovery position is weaker than assumed.

How do you assess value-add potential?

We test rental reversion, lease quality, vacancy, presentation, capital works and alternative configurations against market evidence. The assessment allows for costs, approvals, time and downside scenarios. Potential uplift is an assumption to test, not a guaranteed outcome.

What makes commercial income sustainable?

Rent that the lease and the market both support, from a tenant able to pay it, after the outgoings the owner carries and cannot recover. One-off income, above-market rent and near-term expiries all reduce what the income is worth.

Do you have resources for investors?

Yes. The Commercial Property Investor School is a free set of five short, interactive lessons on yield and value, value-add, leasing and vacancy, refinancing and downside cash flow. There is no sign-up. The lessons are general education, not financial advice.

Can you help if I have already found a property?

Yes. Tell Nick which property you are considering and where the transaction stands. We can discuss a scope covering assessment, comparable evidence, due diligence coordination and negotiation, with the services and fees agreed before work begins.

How much does a commercial buyer’s agent cost?

Fees depend on the acquisition brief and the scope of work. Before appointment, we agree the services, fee basis, payment stages and any minimum fee in writing. The initial strategy session is complimentary and does not appoint Represent Commercial.
