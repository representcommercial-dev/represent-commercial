Questions owners ask

Can you help before the existing tenant leaves?

Yes. We can discuss the current tenant’s intentions and plan the next steps around the lease position, expected availability and access arrangements.

My property is already advertised. Can you review the campaign?

Yes. We can discuss the campaign and identify areas worth examining. Any proposed appointment will take account of existing agency arrangements.

Will you recommend reducing the rent?

Asking rent is one part of the assessment. We also consider presentation, competing stock, availability, enquiry feedback and proposed lease terms.

How do your fees work?

We provide a proposal setting out the leasing scope, commission, marketing costs and any separately proposed management fees before you appoint us.

Can you manage the property once it is leased?

Yes. Ongoing property management can form part of the proposal or a separate appointment.

Can you guarantee when it will lease?

No. Timing depends on the property, demand, pricing and lease terms. We focus on a clear campaign, responsive enquiry handling and recommendations informed by actual feedback.

Which areas do you cover?

This leasing service focuses on Greater Brisbane. Send the property address so we can confirm whether the appointment suits our coverage.
