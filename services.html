Management and leasing, same desk

Vacancy is a management problem before it is a leasing problem

The manager who tracks the expiry, knows the tenant and holds the rent evidence is the one who should be running the leasing. Splitting the two is how a renewal conversation becomes a vacancy, and how a vacancy runs longer than it needed to.

Under management, leasing starts before the space is empty: renewal intent tested early, rent position set against evidence, campaign brief and presentation prepared ahead of the expiry rather than after it.

Common questions

Commercial property management in Brisbane

What does a commercial property manager actually do?

Rent invoicing and collection, arrears, outgoings budgeting and reconciliation, lease administration and critical-date tracking, statutory compliance, maintenance and contractor coordination, inspections and owner reporting. Beyond the administration, the work that changes the result is rent review positioning, renewal strategy, vacancy planning and expenditure decisions.

Can I change managers mid-lease?

Yes. The lease is between you and the tenant; the management appointment is separate and can be changed subject to the notice period in your current management agreement. We handle the transfer of records, trust balances, lease documents and critical dates.

Do you manage property you did not lease or sell?

Yes. Most engagements begin with a property already tenanted — managed elsewhere, or managed by the owner directly.

What does it cost?

Fees are set on an asset-by-asset basis after a review of the property, tenancy profile and current arrangements. Scope varies widely between a single stable tenancy and a multi-tenanted asset with active lease events, so a single percentage quoted before that review would not mean much.
