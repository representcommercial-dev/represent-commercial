PRIVACY policy.

Represent Commercial Pty Ltd handles personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

01

About this Privacy Policy

Represent Commercial Pty Ltd (Represent Commercial, we, us or our) is committed to respecting your privacy and protecting your personal information.

We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and, where applicable, the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, hold, use and disclose personal information in connection with our commercial real estate business and applies to dealings with us in person, by telephone, email, online, through our website or otherwise.

The purpose of this Privacy Policy is to explain:

  • the kinds of personal information we may collect and hold;
  • how and why we collect, use and disclose personal information;
  • how we protect and store personal information;
  • how you may access or seek correction of personal information we hold about you;
  • how you may make a privacy complaint;
  • circumstances in which information may be disclosed to third parties, including overseas recipients; and
  • how we handle information collected through our website and other digital services.

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, personal information, sensitive information and other terms defined by the Privacy Act have the meanings given to them by that Act.

02

Collection of Personal Information

We may collect personal information about current and prospective clients, property owners, landlords, tenants, buyers, sellers, investors, owner-occupiers, occupiers, contractors, suppliers, referral partners, professional advisers, employees, job applicants and other people with whom we deal.

We only collect personal information where it is reasonably necessary for one or more of our business functions or activities.

Depending on the nature of your dealings with us, information we collect may include:

  • name;
  • residential or postal address;
  • email address;
  • telephone and mobile numbers;
  • occupation, employer and business details;
  • company, trust or other entity details;
  • property ownership and property-related information;
  • information concerning property requirements, acquisition criteria or leasing requirements;
  • financial information reasonably required in connection with a property transaction, tenancy, acquisition or other service;
  • identification information where required for legal, regulatory, verification or transaction purposes;
  • information concerning existing or proposed leases, tenancies and property transactions;
  • communications and correspondence with us;
  • marketing and communication preferences;
  • information necessary to manage a client or business relationship; and
  • other information reasonably necessary to provide our services or conduct our business.

We do not ordinarily seek to collect sensitive information. Where sensitive information is required, we will only collect it where reasonably necessary and where permitted by law, including where you have provided consent or collection is required or authorised by law.

How we collect personal information

Where reasonable and practicable, we collect personal information directly from you.

This may occur when you:

  • contact us by telephone, email or through our website;
  • make a property or service enquiry;
  • engage us to provide commercial real estate services;
  • inspect, lease, sell, purchase or otherwise transact in relation to a property;
  • complete a form or provide documentation to us;
  • subscribe to communications or marketing;
  • communicate or meet with us; or
  • otherwise provide information to us.

We may also obtain personal information from third parties and publicly available sources where reasonably necessary for our business activities.

These sources may include:

  • property owners, tenants, purchasers and vendors;
  • real estate agents and agencies;
  • solicitors, accountants, finance brokers and other professional advisers;
  • government agencies and property registers;
  • property information and commercial real estate databases;
  • property listing portals;
  • credit reporting or tenancy information services where applicable;
  • contractors and service providers;
  • referral partners;
  • publicly available corporate, property and professional information; and
  • other sources lawfully available to us.

If we receive unsolicited personal information, we will determine whether we could lawfully have collected that information. If we could not, we will take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify it where lawful and reasonable.

Where lawful and practicable, you may deal with us anonymously or using a pseudonym. However, for many commercial real estate services and transactions we will need to know your identity in order to provide the service or comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

03

Other Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may also collect:

  • telephone or meeting recordings where recording is used for training and monitoring purposes;
  • information contained in job applications;
  • website usage and analytics information;
  • cookies and similar technologies;
  • IP addresses and device information;
  • enquiry and marketing attribution information; and
  • records generated through the software and communication platforms we use to conduct our business.
04

Use of Personal Information

We may use personal information for purposes including:

  • responding to enquiries;
  • providing commercial property management services;
  • providing commercial leasing and lease-related services;
  • assisting clients to acquire or dispose of commercial property;
  • providing buyer-side acquisition and advisory services;
  • providing commercial tenant representation and advisory services;
  • marketing properties for sale or lease;
  • conducting property inspections and managing enquiries;
  • communicating and negotiating with parties involved in transactions;
  • preparing, administering and managing appointments, agreements and other documents;
  • managing client, property, tenant, purchaser, vendor and business relationships;
  • verifying information relevant to a transaction or engagement;
  • obtaining property, title, planning, corporate or other searches;
  • complying with legal, regulatory, licensing, record-keeping and other obligations;
  • processing payments, invoices and expenses;
  • managing contractors and suppliers;
  • maintaining and improving our business systems and services;
  • managing employment and recruitment;
  • marketing our services where permitted by law; and
  • other purposes reasonably connected with our business activities.
05

Disclosure of Personal Information

Where reasonably necessary for our business or services, we may disclose personal information to:

  • property owners, landlords, vendors, purchasers, tenants and prospective tenants;
  • other real estate agents and agencies;
  • solicitors, accountants, valuers, finance brokers and other professional advisers;
  • insurers and insurance brokers;
  • tradespeople, contractors, consultants and property service providers;
  • photographers, marketing providers and advertising agencies;
  • property listing portals and real estate websites;
  • property information, research and commercial real estate data providers;
  • government departments, regulators, courts, tribunals and statutory authorities;
  • land titles and other property registries;
  • banks, payment providers and financial institutions;
  • software, cloud computing, data storage and technology providers;
  • customer relationship management and property management platforms;
  • communications, email and telecommunications providers;
  • analytics and website service providers;
  • organisations involved in acquiring or considering acquiring all or part of our business; and
  • other persons or organisations where you have consented or where disclosure is authorised or required by law.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary for enforcement-related activities undertaken by an authorised enforcement body.

Third-party platforms and service providers may handle information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

06

Overseas Disclosure and Processing

Some technology, cloud, software, analytics and other service providers used by Represent Commercial may store or process information outside Australia.

As a result, personal information may in some circumstances be disclosed to or accessible from overseas jurisdictions.

Where required by the Privacy Act, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that overseas recipients handle personal information consistently with applicable Australian privacy requirements.

The countries in which information is processed may vary depending on the service providers used by us from time to time.

07

Your Privacy Online

Represent Commercial takes reasonable steps to protect information provided through our website and other online services.

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • understand website traffic and usage;
  • improve website functionality and user experience;
  • measure marketing effectiveness;
  • understand how visitors find and interact with our website; and
  • deliver or measure advertising where applicable.

We may use third-party analytics and advertising services, including services provided by Google, Meta and other technology providers.

These third parties may collect information through cookies or similar technologies and may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser and, where available, through relevant privacy or advertising settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. Represent Commercial is not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites, and you should review their privacy policies separately.

08

Marketing

Where permitted by law, Represent Commercial may use personal information to communicate with you about our services, properties, market information and other matters that may be relevant to you.

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by using the unsubscribe mechanism provided in the communication or by contacting us.

We will take reasonable steps to action an opt-out request.

09

Accuracy and Correction of Personal Information

We take reasonable steps to ensure personal information we hold is accurate, complete and up to date having regard to the purposes for which it is used.

If you believe information we hold about you is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date, please contact us and request a correction.

10

Security and Retention

Personal information may be stored electronically or, where applicable, in physical documents.

Represent Commercial takes reasonable steps to protect personal information against misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification and disclosure.

Measures may include:

  • access controls and authentication;
  • appropriate restrictions on access to business systems;
  • secure cloud-based document and data storage;
  • confidentiality obligations applying to employees and contractors;
  • security measures for devices, websites and technology systems; and
  • appropriate physical security for any hard-copy records.

We periodically review information we hold and, where required by law and no longer necessary for a permitted purpose, take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify personal information.

Some records may need to be retained for legal, regulatory, taxation, accounting, insurance, professional or other legitimate business purposes.

11

Access to Personal Information

You may request access to personal information that Represent Commercial holds about you.

Before providing access, we may require reasonable evidence to verify your identity.

We will respond to requests within a reasonable period and in the manner required by applicable privacy law.

In some circumstances permitted by the Privacy Act, we may refuse access or provide only limited access. If we refuse a request where the Privacy Act requires us to provide reasons, we will provide written reasons and information about available complaint mechanisms.

We will not charge you for making an access request. Where permitted by law, we may charge reasonable costs associated with providing access.

12

Privacy Complaints

If you believe Represent Commercial has breached your privacy or the Australian Privacy Principles, please contact us using the details below.

We will consider and investigate privacy complaints and endeavour to respond within a reasonable period.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may be entitled to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Information about privacy complaints and the OAIC is available at www.oaic.gov.au.
