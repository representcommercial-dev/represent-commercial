    How this is calculated

    Queensland transfer duty

    General rates on the higher of the price plus any purchase GST, or the higher dutiable value entered: nil to $5,000; $1.50 per $100 or part above $5,000 to $75,000; $1,050 plus $3.50 per $100 or part above $75,000 to $540,000; $17,325 plus $4.50 per $100 or part above $540,000 to $1,000,000; and $38,025 plus $5.75 per $100 or part above $1,000,000. No residential or other concession is applied. While GST is not yet confirmed, duty excludes GST and is provisional. A manual override replaces the automatic figure everywhere.

    Loan and settlement cash

    Loan = LVR × the lower of the price excluding GST and the valuation. Settlement cash = price + purchase GST + duty + upfront costs − loan. Cash left = available liquid cash − settlement cash, shown as a negative where the fund is short. Reserve shortfall = the reserve less cash left, where positive. Any later GST refund is conditional future cash and is never used to fund settlement.

    Income

    Rent collected = base rent × (1 − vacancy months ÷ 12). Net operating income = rent collected − owner costs − extra vacancy costs, which apply only when vacancy is above 0. Net yield = net operating income ÷ price, adjusted for vacancy, before finance, fund costs and capital expenditure.

    Debt service

    The term in months is the term in years × 12, rounded to the nearest whole month, with a minimum of one month. Principal and interest uses a fixed monthly payment at the annual rate ÷ 12; at 0% the payment is the loan ÷ months. The final payment is capped at the exact outstanding principal plus interest, and the balance carries continuously across all 120 months, so the loan never goes below zero and there are no payments after maturity. Interest only charges monthly interest on the full principal, which is repaid in full in the maturity month. No refinance or extension is assumed. Year one figures are the first 12 months of this schedule.

    Cash flow

    Before contributions = net operating income − debt service − SMSF admin and audit − capital expenditure allowance. After contributions = that figure + net contributions − allocated outflows. All figures are before income tax.

    Ten-year cash flow

    Each year is a 12-month period from acquisition. Rent collected = base rent × (1 + rent growth)year − 1 × (1 − vacancy months ÷ 12). Holding costs = (owner costs after recoveries + extra vacancy costs, where vacancy is above 0 + SMSF admin and audit + capital expenditure allowance) × (1 + holding cost growth)year − 1. Cash flow before contributions = rent collected − holding costs − interest − principal. After contributions adds net contributions and deducts allocated outflows. Vacancy months, the interest rate, contributions and outflows stay constant in every year. Purchase price, GST, duty, upfront costs and any later GST refund are excluded. The figures are not a total investment return or a whole-fund balance, and the 10-year total does not include loan balances.

    Not modelled

    • Income tax, depreciation, capital gains tax, growth and sale.
    • Business real property status, the fund’s investment strategy, related-party rules, contribution caps or eligibility, and lender approval.
    • Financing of GST or costs, lender fees beyond those entered, and any concession, exemption, surcharge or landholder duty.
