Four pathways you can refer into

Refer when

Commercial Property Management

An owner is self-managing, unhappy with their manager's communication, dealing with lease administration problems, or wants more proactive management.

What comes back to you
Any future sale or new leasing of that property. Lease renewals and reviews stay part of our management role.

Refer when

Commercial Leasing

An owner has vacant space, a campaign that has gone stale, an expiry approaching, thin enquiry, or a property that is proving difficult to lease.

What comes back to you
Any future sale of that property.

Refer when

Buyer’s Agency & Acquisition Advisory

A business owner or investor is looking to acquire and wants independent search, analysis, due diligence and negotiation. We hold no sale listings, so there is no stock to steer them into.

What comes back to you
The eventual resale, and any leasing of the asset once acquired.

Refer when

Tenant Representation

A business is approaching lease expiry, relocating, expanding, renewing or renegotiating its premises.

What comes back to you
We act for the occupier only. Any property that client owns, and its sale or leasing, stays yours.
