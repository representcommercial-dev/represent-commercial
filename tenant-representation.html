Leaving the premises

What gets reviewed

  • What the landlord is seeking, item by item, and what each item would cost
  • Practical options, such as agreed works or a payment instead of works, where the landlord will consider them
  • Timing, access and handover planned around the move

Useful to bring

The lease and any variations, condition reports or photographs from the start of the lease, fit-out approvals and drawings, and any make-good schedule or correspondence from the landlord.

Common questions

Tenant representation in Brisbane

What is tenant representation?

A tenant representative acts only for the occupier: defining the requirement, searching on-market and off-market, shortlisting and inspecting, comparing options on total occupancy cost rather than headline rent, negotiating rent, incentive and term, and coordinating the lease documentation with the tenant’s solicitor through to occupation. The Occupancy Cost Comparison Calculator compares up to three premises on effective annual cost, net of incentives and fitout.

How are fees agreed?

Fee structures vary by engagement and are agreed in writing before work begins. Some engagements are fee-for-service to the tenant; in others the fee is met by the landlord. Either way the arrangement is disclosed at the outset. The initial discussion is complimentary. Advice and representation begin under a separately agreed appointment, with scope and fees confirmed in writing.

How early should I start?

Earlier than most occupiers expect. Option dates and notice periods often fall well before expiry, and losing the option removes your main negotiating lever. Where a relocation or fitout is possible, the search, negotiation and works timeline needs to be planned around the expiry rather than compressed into its final months.

Can you help with a renewal alone?

Yes. You do not need to be planning a move. Represent Commercial can confirm your lease dates, test the landlord’s renewal proposal against market evidence and credible alternatives, and negotiate the renewal terms from the tenant’s side.

Can you help if we have already found premises?

Yes. The appointment can start from where you are, covering the work needed from that point, such as assessing the premises, comparing the proposed occupancy costs and negotiating the commercial terms. Scope and fees are confirmed in writing.

Who handles legal documentation?

Agreed terms are recorded in a heads of agreement, then documented in the lease. We advise on the property and commercial terms and coordinate with your solicitor. Your solicitor advises on the legal documents and obligations. For a retail shop lease in Queensland, the landlord must give you a disclosure statement and the proposed lease at least 7 days before you enter into it.
