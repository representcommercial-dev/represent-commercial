How this is calculated

Gross occupancy cost

Area multiplied by net rent plus outgoings, both entered per square metre per annum, plus car parking at the monthly rate you set. Rent and outgoings escalate at the annual review in every year after the first.

Incentive

Months of rent free are valued on the year one net rent and deducted once, in the first year. Incentives structured as a fitout contribution should instead be netted off the fitout cost you enter.

Fitout

The cost you bear, after any landlord contribution. It is included in the total over the term in full. The display toggle only changes whether it is shown as an upfront cost or spread evenly across the term.

Effective annual cost

The total cost over the term divided by the number of years. It is the figure that makes premises of different sizes, rents and incentive structures comparable, and it is the number a tenant should be negotiating on.

What is excluded

Relocation, make-good, business disruption, GST, outgoings reconciliations, fitout depreciation and the time value of money are not modelled. Cash totals are undiscounted. Where two options differ materially in fitout timing, a discounted comparison should be run.
