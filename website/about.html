NICK O'TOOLE

"The person who signs your agreement is the same person reviewing your lease, interrogating your outgoings, and negotiating your rent review."

Nick spent the past 13 years as Asset and Development Manager at Nicolas Malouf Investments, a Brisbane private investment group, and earlier held asset management and analyst roles across commercial portfolios totalling more than $500M.

Across that career he has negotiated more than $20M in lease transactions as a client-side asset manager — acting for the owner, not as an agent.

He holds a Bachelor of Commerce (Finance) and a Bachelor of Business Management (Real Estate and Development) from the University of Queensland, and a full Queensland real estate licence.
