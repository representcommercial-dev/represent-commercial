What Nick reviews

An owner-side view of where the property stands.

A Commercial Property Performance Review is an initial review of a commercial property's current position from the owner's side of the table. It is personally conducted by Nick O'Toole, Principal and Licensee of Represent Commercial, for owners across Brisbane and South East Queensland.

Depending on the property and the issue, Nick may consider:

  • Current leasing position
  • Asking rent and commercial terms
  • Available market evidence
  • Competing stock
  • Vacancy duration
  • Enquiry and inspection friction
  • Property presentation
  • Advertising and information quality
  • Incentives
  • Occupancy costs and outgoings, where relevant
  • Lease expiry and tenant position
  • Management and reporting issues
  • Immediate decision points

Common questions

About the Performance Review

Is the Commercial Property Performance Review free?

Yes. The initial review and the phone discussion with Nick are free, and there is no obligation to appoint Represent Commercial.

What does the review include?

Nick reviews the property, its current position and the information you provide, then discusses with you by phone what appears to deserve attention and the practical next steps. It is not a valuation, a formal written report, due diligence, or legal or tax advice.

Do I need to send documents?

No. Documents are optional. If you have a current lease, leasing proposal, tenancy schedule or outgoings information, it can help, but you can request the review without them.

What happens after I submit the form?

Nick personally reviews your request and will make contact within one business day to arrange the discussion.

Do I need to change property managers or leasing agents?

No. The review is independent of any decision to change agents. It may show that the current arrangement is working, or it may identify issues your current agent can address.

Can you review a property outside Brisbane?

Represent Commercial works with owners across Brisbane and South East Queensland. If your property is elsewhere, include the address and Nick will tell you whether he can help.
