Common questions

Questions owners ask

What exactly will I receive?

A formal written report outlining your property’s current position, three priorities and recommended next steps. After receiving your report, you can discuss the findings with Nick by phone or video call.

Is the property review really free?

Yes. The Commercial Property Performance Review is free. If you choose to engage Represent Commercial for further services afterwards, we’ll agree the scope and fees with you before proceeding.

What information do I need to provide?

Start with the property address. A lease or tenancy summary and recent income and outgoings help if you have them. Nick will ask for anything else once the scope is agreed.

Is a site inspection included?

Whether an inspection is needed is confirmed as part of the scope for your property.

Can I retain my current property manager?

Yes. The review looks at the property’s position before any change is recommended. The right outcome may be to continue the current approach, adjust one part of it or take no immediate action.

Are ongoing services optional?

Yes. Asset management, property management and leasing are separate services, scoped and agreed only if you choose to proceed. Implementation is not included in the review.

Where do you work?

Asset management is available throughout Queensland. Property management and leasing are available in Brisbane.

Is a Property Performance Review a valuation?

No. It is not a valuation, building inspection, engineering report, or legal, tax or financial advice. Where one of those is needed, the review will say so.
