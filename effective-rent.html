How this is calculated

Face rent over the term

Area multiplied by the face net rent, escalating at the annual review in every year after the first, summed across the term.

Incentive value

Only rent free and abatement reduce the rent payable. A fitout contribution or other cash incentive is a payment to the tenant, not a rent offset, so it lowers the effective rent and the total over the term without changing what is invoiced in any year. Rent free is valued at the year one net rent for the months granted. Abatement is entered as a percentage of the year one rent. The resulting dollar amount is applied unchanged in every year of the term and is not recalculated against the reviewed rent, so it does not grow as the face rent escalates. A 30 per cent abatement on a $200,000 year one rent is $60,000 a year for the whole term. Fitout contributions and other incentives are taken at the cash amount you enter. The total is deducted from the face rent over the term.

Gross or net

Where a rent is quoted gross, one figure covers rent and outgoings together and no separate outgoings entry applies. Where it is quoted net, the outgoings are entered separately and the gross rent is the sum of the two.

Effective rent

The face rent over the term less the total incentive, divided by the term and the area. On a net basis it is reported as a net effective rent, and on a gross basis as a gross effective rent. It is the rent the deal actually produces, and the figure that belongs in a comparable-evidence file — face rents with undisclosed incentives are the most common source of mispriced leasing evidence.

Discounted effective rent

The same calculation with each year’s cash flow discounted at the rate you enter, then annualised. Annual rents are discounted at the mid-point of each year, and a fitout contribution or other cash incentive is treated as paid at the mid-point of year one. That is the only timing assumption in the calculation; a contribution paid later in the term is worth less than shown. Because incentives are concentrated at the start of a term, the discounted figure is usually lower than the undiscounted one, which matters when comparing a large upfront contribution against rent free spread over years.

What is excluded

Outgoings appear only in the gross comparison and are assumed fully recoverable. Leasing fees, incentive amortisation for accounting purposes, GST, make-good, tenant covenant and any clawback on early termination are not modelled. Incentive treatment for tax and accounting should be confirmed with your adviser.

Face rent is the number in the lease. Effective rent is the deal.

Comparable evidence quoted on face rents without the incentive behind it will mislead an owner and a tenant equally. Represent negotiates and records both sides of that number.
