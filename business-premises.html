BUSINESS PREMISES · BRISBANE

SHOULD YOUR BUSINESS RENEW, RELOCATE OR buy?

Which option gives your business the right space, cost and flexibility? An approaching lease expiry, changing space requirement or potential purchase affects capital, operations and long-term flexibility. This guide sets out what each pathway depends on and how they compare, for established businesses in Brisbane and South East Queensland.

CONSIDERATIONS, NOT RECOMMENDATIONS

WHAT EACH PATH DEPENDS on.

The right course depends on the business, the market and the premises available. These are the questions to work through for each path.

Renew

  • Existing premises still suit operations.
  • Assess the renewal against market evidence and alternatives.
  • Check option dates, reviews, outgoings and make-good.
Renewing your leaseRent review calculator

Relocate

  • Space, access, location or layout no longer supports the business.
  • Compare total occupancy costs and operational implications.
  • Consider fit-out, timing, overlap rent and disruption.
Relocating your businessRenewal vs relocation calculator

Buy

  • Suitable premises, funding and the business’s capital priorities warrant assessment.
  • Test operational fit, ownership costs and future flexibility.
  • Involve the appropriate finance, tax and legal advisers.
Buying business premisesLease vs Buy Calculator
COMMON TRIGGERS

WHEN THE PREMISES DECISION COMES INTO focus.

  • The current lease is approaching an option or expiry date
  • The business has outgrown or underutilises its space
  • Rent, outgoings or make-good exposure are increasing
  • The location or property no longer supports operations
  • The business is considering purchasing through an operating entity, property entity or SMSF
  • The business needs greater security or flexibility
