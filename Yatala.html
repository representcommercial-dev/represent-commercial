YATALA.
GOLD COAST–BRISBANE
CORRIDOR.

SEQ's large-format industrial corridor. Where scale meets access.

Yatala occupies the midpoint of the Pacific Motorway (M1) between Brisbane and the Gold Coast — the spine of SEQ's freight and manufacturing network. Large land parcels, access to key heavy-vehicle freight routes, and a tenant profile built for the long term.

Precinct Overview

WHY YATALA.

01
Location
M1 Corridor
Strategically positioned on the Pacific Motorway between Brisbane and the Gold Coast — a precinct able to serve both markets from one location.
02
Access
M1 Direct
Direct Pacific Motorway (M1) access with dual carriageway frontage, well connected to key heavy-vehicle freight routes.
03
Precinct type
Large Format
Predominantly freestanding warehouses and manufacturing facilities on large land parcels. Low site coverage ratios allow future expansion — a key tenant retention lever.
04
Demand driver
Dual-Market Draw
Brisbane and Gold Coast growth funnels through this corridor. Food manufacturing, construction materials, and FMCG distribution all concentrate here for the same reason.
