The acquisition process

From business requirements to the right purchase.

  1. 01 · DEFINE

    Set the premises brief

    Agree a written brief covering operational fit, access, parking, power, services and growth requirements, with your adviser-confirmed budget, preferred locations and timing.

  2. 02 · SEARCH

    Find and compare

    Search and compare properties against the brief, including on-market opportunities and appropriate direct approaches.

  3. 03 · ASSESS

    Test the purchase

    Assess comparable sales, market rent, outgoings, condition, capital expenditure, flood, planning and strata constraints, and future leasing and resale flexibility.

  4. 04 · ACQUIRE

    Negotiate and coordinate

    Negotiate price and terms, then coordinate with your SMSF adviser, accountant, lender and solicitor through to settlement.

Technical, legal, planning and other specialist conclusions come from your solicitor and the relevant consultants. Business relocation, fit-out and construction are not part of a standard buyer’s agency appointment. Fees are agreed in writing before work begins.

Common questions

Buying premises through an SMSF

Can my business lease premises owned by my SMSF?

Potentially, where the property qualifies as business real property and the arrangement meets the applicable SMSF rules. Related-party leases must be on arm’s length terms and reflect market rent. Your SMSF adviser and solicitor should confirm the proposed arrangement before you proceed.

Do I need an SMSF already set up?

You can speak with us before a fund is established. Your qualified advisers need to determine whether an SMSF is appropriate and confirm the required structure before you enter a purchase contract.

How much can I spend?

Your budget depends on your fund’s position, acquisition costs, liquidity needs and any lender requirements. Your SMSF adviser and broker establish the financial parameters. We then search and assess property within the agreed brief.

I have already found a property. Can you still help?

Yes. We can discuss a scope covering assessment, negotiation and transaction coordination for an identified property. It helps to make contact before you commit to a price or sign a contract.

How are fees agreed?

Fees depend on whether you need a full search and acquisition service or assistance with a property you have already identified. We explain the scope, engagement fee and acquisition fee in a written proposal before you appoint us. The initial strategy session is complimentary and does not appoint Represent Commercial.
