How this is calculated

Occupancy cost

Each option is area multiplied by net rent plus outgoings, escalating at the annual review in every year after the first. Renewal uses your current area; relocation uses the new area, so a change in floor plate is captured.

Incentives

Months of rent free are valued on the year one net rent of the relevant premises and deducted once. Incentives taken as a fitout contribution should be netted off the fitout cost you enter rather than added as rent free.

One-off costs

Renewal carries any upgrade you fund. Relocation carries fitout net of contribution, relocation costs, the make-good on the premises you are leaving, and overlap rent for the months you hold both. Make-good is charged only to relocation, on the basis that a renewal ordinarily defers it.

Break-even rent

The net rent per square metre on the new premises at which relocating costs the same as renewing, holding every other input constant. It is the number to take into a negotiation with the incoming landlord.

What is excluded

Business disruption, productivity effects, staff attrition, the time value of money, fitout depreciation and tax treatment are not modelled. Cash totals are undiscounted. A relocation with heavy upfront capital will look better on an undiscounted basis than it does on a discounted one.
