Questions owners ask

What is fractional asset management?

Senior asset management bought by the project rather than by salary. You get the strategic oversight an institutional owner has in-house: a plan for the asset, direction for the property manager, approval of capital works and leadership of lease events.

Will you try to replace my property manager?

No. We oversee your property manager; we do not take over the management. If the arrangement is not working we will tell you plainly, but the engagement is not a pitch for the management appointment.

Do you work outside Queensland?

No. Our asset management service is currently available throughout Queensland only.

How are fees set?

Each engagement is scoped as a project. The scope, deliverables and fee are agreed in writing before work begins.

What size of owner is this for?

Syndicators, family offices and private owners holding one or more commercial assets who need senior oversight but cannot justify a full-time asset manager.
