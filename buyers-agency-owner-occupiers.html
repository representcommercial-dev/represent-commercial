From business requirements to the right purchase.

  1. 01 · DEFINE

    Set the premises brief

    Agree a written premises brief based on your operational requirements, preferred locations, budget and timing, including existing lease commitments and any planned relocation.

  2. 02 · SEARCH

    Find and compare suitable options

    Search and shortlist properties against the agreed brief, including on-market opportunities and appropriate direct approaches.

  3. 03 · ASSESS

    Test the property and the purchase

    Assess operational fit, market evidence, ownership costs and future flexibility. Coordinate specialist investigations with your solicitor and relevant consultants.

  4. 04 · ACQUIRE

    Negotiate and coordinate the purchase

    Negotiate price and commercial terms, coordinate acquisition milestones and work alongside your advisers through to settlement.

Common questions

Buying premises for your business

Should my business lease or buy its premises?

It depends on total cost over the intended period of occupation, the capital tied up, flexibility if the business grows or moves, and whether the property would still suit another occupier. The Lease vs Buy guide and calculator set out how to compare the two before committing.

Can my business lease premises owned by my SMSF?

Potentially, where the property qualifies as business real property and the arrangement meets the applicable SMSF rules. Related-party leases must be on arm’s length terms and reflect market rent. Your SMSF adviser and solicitor should confirm the proposed arrangement before you proceed.

Do I need an SMSF already set up?

You can speak with us before a fund is established. Your qualified advisers need to determine whether an SMSF is appropriate and confirm the required structure before you enter a purchase contract. To estimate the cash an SMSF would need for a Queensland purchase and test cash flow under different borrowing, cost and vacancy assumptions, use the SMSF commercial property acquisition calculator.

Will you work with my accountant and broker?

Yes. We work alongside your existing advisers so the property search and transaction reflect the requirements they establish. Represent provides property acquisition services, rather than financial, tax, legal or credit advice.

Can you help if I have already found a property?

Yes. Tell Nick which property you are considering and where the transaction stands. We can discuss a scope covering assessment, comparable evidence, due diligence coordination and negotiation, with the services and fees agreed before work begins.

How much does a commercial buyer’s agent cost?

Fees depend on the acquisition brief and the scope of work. Before appointment, we agree the services, fee basis, payment stages and any minimum fee in writing. The initial strategy session is complimentary and does not appoint Represent Commercial.
