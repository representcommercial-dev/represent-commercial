Before you enquire

A few practical questions

Can you help if I’ve already found a property?

Yes. The engagement can focus on assessing the premises, comparing the proposed occupancy costs and negotiating commercial terms. The scope is agreed to suit your position.

Do you help with lease renewals?

Yes. We can assess the renewal proposal and compare it with alternatives so you can make an informed decision about staying or relocating. Let Nick know your option and notice deadlines early.

What does tenant representation cost?

Fees depend on the agreed scope. The fee structure, who pays and any commission arrangements are disclosed and agreed in writing before work begins.

Who do you represent?

When appointed as your tenant representative, we act for you as the occupier. The engagement and any relevant fee arrangements are clarified at the outset.

Does this include legal advice?

We advise on the property and commercial terms and coordinate with your solicitor. Your solicitor advises on the legal documents and obligations.
