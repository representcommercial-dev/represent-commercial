Owners

02

Secure & transition

Progress the preferred solution and coordinate the commercial transition.

Principal & Licensee

NICK O'TOOLE

Nick O’Toole brings more than 20 years of owner-side commercial property experience across asset management, leasing, acquisitions, development and portfolio strategy.

Before founding Represent Commercial, Nick spent more than two decades advising private property owners and managing commercial property portfolios. This included 13 years as Asset and Development Manager at Nicolas Malouf Investments, a Brisbane-based private investment group, following earlier roles spanning acquisitions, asset management, development, leasing and property analysis across the industrial, office and retail sectors.

Today, Nick applies that experience across Represent Commercial’s three service lines: buyer-side acquisition advisory, property management and leasing, and commercial tenant representation.

Whether acting for a buyer, owner or occupier, his role is the same: to provide clear advice, rigorous analysis and commercially focused representation from someone sitting exclusively on the client’s side of the table.

Nick holds a Bachelor of Commerce (Finance) and a Bachelor of Business Management (Real Estate and Development) from the University of Queensland and a full Queensland real estate agent licence.
